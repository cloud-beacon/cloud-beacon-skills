01The opportunity

One payment run, one transmission path.

Shamrock is implementing a Treasury Automation Suite — BankFabric — in D365 Finance, and needs a hands-on architect to carry it end to end: ISV deployment, banking integrations, payment automation, technical configuration, testing, and production release, working directly with Finance and Treasury.

Three things must be true at go-live: the outbound AP bank-file logic BankFabric replaces is disabled or bypassed with no duplicate transmission path left behind; transmission success and failure are monitored, not assumed; and connectivity, allowlisting, and credential ownership are confirmed.

By go-live

Legacy AP bank-file logic retired, BankFabric validated across every environment, payment files regression-tested, and a monitoring story Treasury actually trusts.

After hypercare

One transmission path, alerting on success and failure, credential and allowlisting ownership confirmed — and a Finance team that runs it without us.
